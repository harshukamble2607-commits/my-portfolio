ADD YOUR RESUME HERE
====================

1. Put your resume PDF in this folder, for example:

     public/resume/Harshvardhan-Kamble-Resume.pdf

2. Open  src/data/profile.js  and set:

     resumeUrl: 'resume/Harshvardhan-Kamble-Resume.pdf'

   (The path is relative to the public folder, so do NOT include "public/".)

3. That's it. The "Resume" button in the navigation becomes a real download
   link automatically. Until a path is set, the button shows a clear
   "coming soon" notice instead of linking to a missing file.

Delete this placeholder text file once your PDF is in place.
